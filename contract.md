## 1. SCOPE OF WORK

The Supplier shall provide the following goods and services to the Buyer:

- **Product:** High-performance semiconductor chips
- **Specifications:** 7nm process technology, 64-core architecture
- **Quantity:** 10,000 units per month
- **Quality Standards:** ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing

## 2. TERMS AND CONDITIONS

### 2.1 Delivery Schedule
- **Initial Delivery:** February 1, 2025
- **Monthly Deliveries:** First business day of each month
- **Delivery Location:** Buyer's warehouse at 123 Innovation Drive, San Francisco, CA

### 2.2 Payment Terms
- **Unit Price:** $150.00 USD per unit
- **Total Contract Value:** $18,000,000.00 USD
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from invoice date
- **Late Payment Penalty:** 1.5% per month on overdue amounts

### 2.3 Quality Assurance
- **Warranty Period:** 24 months from delivery date
- **Acceptance Criteria:** 99.9% functionality rate
- **Testing Requirements:** Full functional testing before shipment
- **Rejection Process:** 48-hour notification for defective products

## 3. RISK MANAGEMENT

### 3.1 Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures due to circumstances beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to:
- Natural disasters
- Government actions
- Labor disputes
- Supply chain disruptions

### 3.2 Indemnification
The Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless the Buyer from any claims arising from:
- Product defects
- Intellectual property violations
- Safety incidents
- Regulatory non-compliance

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 4.1 Ownership
- **Existing IP:** Each party retains ownership of pre-existing intellectual property
- **New IP:** Joint development IP shall be jointly owned
- **Licenses:** Non-exclusive, worldwide license for contract performance

### 4.2 Confidentiality
- **Term:** 5 years from contract expiration
- **Scope:** All technical, business, and financial information
- **Breach Penalty:** $500,000 per violation

## 5. TERMINATION

### 5.1 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice for:
- Material breach of contract terms
- Bankruptcy or insolvency
- Failure to meet quality standards
- Regulatory violations

### 5.2 Early Termination Fee
- **Buyer Termination:** 3 months of average monthly order value
- **Supplier Termination:** 6 months of average monthly order value

## 6. COMPLIANCE

### 6.1 Regulatory Requirements
- **Environmental:** EPA standards compliance
- **Safety:** OSHA workplace safety standards
- **Labor:** Fair Labor Standards Act compliance
- **Export:** ITAR and EAR compliance
